Terms of Service

These terms explain how orders, delivery, and food-safety requests work when you use FoodieCart.

  1. Orders and cancellations

    Once you confirm an order, the kitchen may begin preparing it immediately. You may request a cancellation within 60 seconds of placing the order. After that period, cancellation may not be possible because ingredients and preparation time have already been committed.

  2. Delivery estimates

    Delivery times are estimates based on kitchen activity, courier availability, and local conditions. We work to deliver your order promptly and safely; unexpected delays may affect arrival time.

  3. Courier hand-off

    Please keep your contact details available when your courier arrives. If you cannot be reached after reasonable contact attempts, the courier may leave the order in a suitable safe place. Food left unattended may be affected by temperature, weather, or other conditions.

  4. Allergies and dietary needs

    Restaurant partners prepare food in their own kitchens, where ingredients and equipment may be shared. If you have a severe allergy, contact the restaurant directly before ordering so it can confirm whether your request can be safely accommodated.

  5. Delivery and service fees

    Delivery fees and any applicable surcharges can vary by distance, demand, and weather. The charges for your order are shown at checkout before you confirm payment.
